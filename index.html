Our Story

Fueling the Creative Passion of Hetauda's Art Community

Historically, artists and art students in Hetauda had to rely on limited general stationery shops or travel all the way to Kathmandu for specialized tools.

**Art Lab** was established to fill this gap. We are Hetauda’s first dedicated fine art station—designed to empower visual artists, calligraphers, architects, hobbyists, and DIY crafters with top-tier tools, professional pigments, and specialized canvas media.

"Art is not what you see, but what you make others see. We provide the finest tools to make that vision real."
